# Leer Academie Breda: product context

## What it is
The learning platform for gemeente Breda. Employees find and follow trainings, managers keep their team's required learning on track, and the Leren & Ontwikkelen (L&O) team runs the offer.

## Who uses it
- **Medewerker** (municipal employee): finishes required trainings, discovers new ones, sees their own progress.
- **Manager** (team lead): sees team progress, sends reminders, recommends trainings, runs the weekly "leerminuut".
- **L&O**: manages the offer, campaigns and reporting across the organisation.

Most people open it on a work laptop during office hours, between other tasks. Many also use AFAS InSite every day, so familiar patterns beat novelty.

## Voice
Dutch, informal ("je/jij"), short sentences. Name the action, not the feature: "Rond je verplichte training af", "3 open acties".

## Surface mode
Operate (impeccable): people come to finish a task. Scanability, consistency and accessibility outrank expression.

## Constraints
- WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion respected.
- Breda red is the only accent and is reserved for action and attention.
- No official gemeente logo files; use the placeholder wordmark.
- Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (the existing stack of this repo).
